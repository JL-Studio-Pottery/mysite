Bough Pots

Bough pots originated in 18th-century Europe and were designed to display cut branches or flowers. They combine aesthetic appeal with functional elegance.

Cache Pots

Cache pots, from the French word "cacher" (to hide), are decorative outer containers for plant pots. They add an elegant touch to interiors while concealing plain planters.
